Patient carry over a full year, walk-forward

The honest split

The rules were designed while studying the last three months. Everything before that is data the rules never saw, so it is the cleanest estimate of what to expect.

The rules

Every decision every 8 hours uses only data available at that moment.

  1. Crypto only. Perp-perp across venues and same-venue cash-and-carry. Tokenized stocks and commodities are skipped: their funding jumps with market hours.
  2. Liquid markets only: both legs must have averaged $5M a day over the prior 30 days, measured at the time. Two positions at most, $5,000 a slot: $5,000 notional per perp-perp leg at 2x, or $3,333 spot plus a 2x short for carry. One position per asset.
  3. Enter on persistent spreads only. Positive carry in at least 60% of the last 7 days' 8h windows and in each of the last 3, and expected net over 7 days above 10 bps after costs, using half the measured spread.
  4. Exit only when staying costs more than leaving. Close once the 7-day average carry, projected over a week, would lose more than the fees and slippage of closing. Hold at least 3 days.
  5. Half the fills as post-only limit orders (maker fee, no slippage); the rest cross the book.
  6. Collateral stays on each venue. Top up a leg from the other when it falls to half its margin or 1.5x maintenance; trim both legs if a rally inflates notional 50% over target.

How close to real it is

No hindsight: the rules are frozen exactly as designed; every decision every 8 hours uses only funding, prices and volume up to that moment.

Point-in-time universe: a pair is tradable only once both legs averaged at least $5M a day over the previous 30 days, measured then, not today. Markets that were liquid earlier and faded are included.

Funding: every settled print from each venue, from OKX's and Gate's monthly archives and the live APIs.

Slippage: walked through today's order books at the trade size, then scaled for each date by the square root of today's volume over that date's 30-day volume (thinner markets cost more), priced separately at entry and exit.

Fees, transfers, gas, margin: base-tier fees, half the fills as maker; $1 withdrawal plus deposit gas and a 2 bp USDT/USDC swap per transfer; hourly margin checks against venue maintenance rates, top-ups, trims and liquidation.

What is still not real

Venues: OKX, Gate, Hyperliquid and dYdX only. Bitget keeps 90 days of funding and its archive is unreachable from here; Binance and Bybit are blocked here.

Delisted markets are missing, since the universe starts from what is listed today. Hyperliquid prices are 4-hourly before the last ~208 days, so older margin checks are coarser.

Fills: maker fills are assumed to fill at the touch with no queue risk; both legs fill at the same moment. Historical order books are not public, so slippage is modelled, not measured.

Backtest, not trading advice.
