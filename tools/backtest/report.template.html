Funding-rate arbitrage with $10,000, the last three months

    Equity, $10,000 start

    Marked every 8 hours, including open positions' funding, price P&L and costs paid so far. Hover for values.

    Where the money went

    Funding collected against everything it cost to collect it. Price P&L is what the two legs' prices did against each other (basis), since direction is hedged.

    Every scenario

    Same data, same costs. Only the base case's rules were fixed before seeing results; the variants show how much the answer moves with each choice, and the hindsight rows show what perfect foresight of the best pairs would have paid.

    Trades

    How the strategy trades

    Every 8 hours (00:00, 08:00, 16:00 UTC) it looks only at data up to that moment. A pair qualifies when its funding carry was positive in at least 60% of the last 21 windows (7 days), positive in each of the last 3, and its expected carry over the next 7 days (half the latest window, half the 7-day mean) beats the round-trip cost plus transfers by the entry hurdle.

    Capital is split into slots (3 in the base case); each slot holds one pair, one asset at most once. A position exits when its carry over the last 24 hours averages below zero (48 hours with the slow exit) or the forward estimate turns negative, then the slot refills.

    Perp-perp: each $3,333 slot puts half on each venue as margin at 2x, so $3,333 notional a leg. Cash-and-carry: spot plus a 2x short on the same venue, $2,222 notional.

    What is charged

    Funding: the rates each venue actually settled, every print, on the position's live notional.

    Fees: base-tier taker on entry and exit of both legs (OKX 0.05%, Bitget 0.06%, Gate 0.075%, Hyperliquid 0.045%, dYdX 0.05%; spot 0.1 to 0.2%). Maker variants use maker fees on half the fills, with no slippage on those.

    Slippage: average fill versus mid from walking each venue's order book at the trade size, entry and exit sides separately.

    Transfers and gas: funding each venue costs a $1 withdrawal fee, plus deposit gas of $0.05 (Arbitrum bridge to Hyperliquid) or $0.10 (to dYdX), plus 2 bps to swap USDT to USDC for those two. Every margin top-up between venues pays this again. Trading itself has no gas on any of these venues (Hyperliquid and dYdX run their own chains with gasless orders).

    Margin: each perp leg is checked hourly against its venue's maintenance rate. When a leg's equity falls to half its margin or 1.5x maintenance, both legs' equity is pooled and split (one transfer). When a rally pushes notional 50% over target, both legs are trimmed back, paying fees and slippage on the trim. A liquidation would forfeit the leg's remaining margin; none occurred.

    Universe

    Limits

    Binance and Bybit, the largest venues, were unreachable from the test machine and are not in it. Their rates often set one side of the best spreads.

    Slippage comes from today's order books; three months ago some books (BTW especially, which rose about 14x) were thinner, so early costs are likely understated.

    The universe is markets that are liquid today, which favours survivors. Reverse carry is excluded: historical margin-borrow rates are not public.

    Hourly prices miss intra-hour wicks, so margin stress is understated. Fills are assumed instant on both legs; in practice the legs fill seconds apart.

    Backtest, not trading advice. Past funding does not predict future funding.
